Hi, I'm Caroline.

Cleaning has always been more than a job to me. It's something I've enjoyed for as long as I can remember.

I started cleaning houses as a teenager. My mother believed in a truly clean and organized home, so I learned early about attention to detail and the right techniques for the toughest jobs, from stubborn stains to hard-to-clean household items. I still use those tips and methods today.

I grew up playing three competitive sports (soccer, volleyball, and softball) and went on to play soccer in college. That taught me discipline, dedication, and to give everything my best.

Professionally, I spent 10 years as a social worker before stepping away to care for my family. Those years taught me patience, compassion, and how to help people without judgment.

Along the way I discovered that I love being hands-on, staying busy, and seeing the results of hard work right away. There's something deeply satisfying about walking into a space that needs attention and leaving it clean, organized, and refreshed.

I especially love challenging projects, including heavily cluttered and hoarded homes. Everyone deserves a clean, comfortable space they can feel good about, and I find real fulfillment in helping people reclaim their homes, without judgment.

Caroline's Cleaning Crew is a licensed, insured, owner-operated business, so you'll always be talking directly with me. My goal is simple: treat every home with care, take pride in every detail, and leave every space better than I found it.

"I treat every home as if it were my own."

Caroline
