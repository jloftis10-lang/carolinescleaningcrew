What's included

Open a room to see the checklist.

Kitchen
  • Clean countertops, stovetop, and microwave
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and backsplash
  • Wipe the outside of the fridge
  • Empty trash and replace the liner
  • Sweep and mop floors

Inside the fridge, inside the oven, and inside cabinets and drawers are optional add-ons.

Bathrooms
  • Clean and sanitize toilets
  • Scrub showers and bathtubs
  • Clean sinks and faucets
  • Clean countertops and vanities
  • Clean mirrors and glass
  • Wipe down cabinet fronts
  • Empty trash
  • Sweep and mop floors
Bedrooms
  • Make or straighten beds
  • Dust lamps, nightstands, and decor
  • Dust furniture and surfaces
  • Clean mirrors
  • Empty trash
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
Living areas
  • Dust furniture, surfaces, light fixtures, and ceiling fans
  • Wipe down tables and shelves
  • Dust window sills and baseboards
  • Clean mirrors and glass surfaces
  • Vacuum couches
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Sweep and mop floors
  • Empty trash

Interior windows, baseboard detailing, blinds or shutters, carpet steam cleaning, and upholstery cleaning are optional add-ons.

Throughout the home
  • Remove fingerprints from light switches and door handles
  • Spot clean walls and doors
  • Dust accessible surfaces
  • Straighten pillows and decor
  • General tidying and straightening
