Good questions

Frequently asked

How much does a cleaning cost?

We keep our rates budget-friendly. Recurring cleans (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) start at $100. One-time, deep, and move-out cleans depend on the size of your home, so send a quote request or give Caroline a call for a clear price, no surprises.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

Nope. We bring our own supplies and equipment. If you prefer we use specific products in your home, just let us know.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Many clients give us a key or door code. Whatever works best for you is fine with us.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Let us know within 24 hours and we'll come back to make it right.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Just give Caroline a call at (760) 492-9338. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice when possible.
