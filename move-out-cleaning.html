Good questions

Common questions

How much does a move-in or move-out clean cost?

Move cleans are quoted per job. Tell Caroline the size of the home, the date, and any add-ons you'd like, and she'll send you a quote.

What should I include in my request?

Your ZIP code, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, your move date, and anything that needs extra attention. You can add details in the quote form or just call or text.

Does sending a request book a cleaning?

No. A request lets Caroline know what you need. She'll get back to you with a quote, and nothing is scheduled until you both agree on a day and time.
